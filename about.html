HELLO, I'M MARIELLE

A simple person with meaningful dreams.

My name is Marielle Joyce Maniego, and chess is one of the activities that makes my University Meet journey meaningful. As a chess player, I enjoy every challenge that tests my patience, focus, and decision-making skills.

Every game is an opportunity to learn, improve, and discover new strategies. Win or lose, each match teaches me valuable lessons about discipline, sportsmanship, and perseverance.

My journey is not only about winning medals or defeating opponents, but also about creating unforgettable memories, building friendships, and representing my school with pride. Every move brings a new lesson, and every game becomes part of my story.
